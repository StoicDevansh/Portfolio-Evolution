01 · About

AR

I came to AI systems through distributed systems, not the other way around — which means I ask why something fails before I ask how to build it. The model is rarely the hard part. The hard part is what surrounds it: the routing logic, the concurrency model, the observability that tells you what actually broke and when.

Most recently I've been focused on inference infrastructure — building the control planes that decide which backend handles a request, under what load, and what happens when one goes down. I care about systems that perform the same in production as they do in a demo, which is rarer than it should be.

What I don't do

  • Ship API wrappers dressed up as products
  • Build for its own sake — the system has to earn what it costs to run
  • Take work that's just a search away from being solved already
